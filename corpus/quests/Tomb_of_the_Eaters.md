# Quest: Tomb of the Eaters

- **Factions:** Barathrumites
- **Reputation:** 100
- **Level:** 30
- **Achievement:** ACH_EATER_TOMB

## Accomplishment

> You returned from the Tomb of the Eaters after successfully disabling the Spindle's magnetic field.

## Steps (6)

### Step 1: Recover the Mark of Death

_XP: 2500_

> Recover the lost sigil used by the Eaters to mark their dead.

### Step 2: Inscribe the Mark

_XP: 2500_

> Tattoo the mark on your body.

### Step 3: Enter the Tomb of the Eaters

_XP: 2500_

> Gain entrance to the sealed Tomb.

### Step 4: Ascend the Tomb and Cross into Brightsheol

_XP: 2500_

> Ascend the Tomb and find Resheph's burial chamber. From there, discover a way to travel to Brightsheol.

### Step 5: Disable the Spindle's Magnetic Field

_XP: 2500_

> Access the Spindle's control unit and turn off the field.

### Step 6: Return to Grit Gate

_XP: 7500_

> Return to Grit Gate and speak to Barathrum.
